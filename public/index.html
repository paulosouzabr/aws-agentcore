How scoring works

Every bumper your ball strikes adds points, and each bumper gets hotter (worth more) the faster the ball was moving on impact. Keep a rally alive with your flippers to chain hits before the ball drains. You get three balls per round; your final score is the total earned across all three. A clean game over submits straight to the high-score table below.
